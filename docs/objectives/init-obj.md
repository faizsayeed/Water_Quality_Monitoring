Objectives of the Project : 
1.	To monitor water quality by collecting and analyzing important water parameters. 
2.	To detect water pollution using parameters such as pH, turbidity, temperature, and dissolved oxygen. 
3.	To use Machine Learning algorithms to predict whether the water is safe or unsafe. 
4.	To apply statistical analysis to identify patterns and changes in water quality. 
5.	To provide early warnings when water quality becomes unsafe. 
6.	To help users and authorities make better decisions for water management. 
7.	To reduce manual testing by creating an automated water quality monitoring system.
